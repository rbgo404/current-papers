## Short note

Controlled recovery scenarios separate cases requiring retry, switching to an alternative tool, or recognizing that no viable path remains. Version 2 uses the Bench2Robust title; version 1 was titled Retry, Switch, or Abstain? Learning Strategy-Aware Tool-Use Policies via Controlled Error Injection.
