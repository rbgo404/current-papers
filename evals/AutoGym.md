# AutoGym: Blueprint-First Generation of Verifiable Agent Gyms

- **Authors:** Aarati Andrea Noronha, Kavya Ravikumar, and Carly Xiaoyu Lin
- **arXiv:** [2609.22592](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22592)
- **PDF:** [Original on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.22592)
- **License:** [arXiv non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html)

## Short note

AutoGym generates tasks, executable environments, and verifiers from a blueprint that defines valid solutions and verification criteria before constructing the environment. Its controls and performance-informed curriculum aim to keep task difficulty aligned with model capabilities.
