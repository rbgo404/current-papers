## Short note

Studies large-scale n-gram language models for machine translation, with distributed training and the Stupid Backoff smoothing method. Here “large language models” refers to pre-neural statistical models.

## Citation note

This is the “Brants+ 2007” reference in Stanford CS336 Lecture 1's pre-neural language-modeling overview.
